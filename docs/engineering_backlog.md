### Medium Priority
- [ ] **Fallback: split a demo into two chained passes instead of merging (Scenario 2, raised 2026-08-18):** When two highlights' *recording windows* genuinely overlap they can't be separate takes — you can't run two `mirv_recordmovie` sessions at once — so `build_batch_queue` merges them into one continuous take.
